📚 How to Wholesale

  1. 🔍 Hunt for motivated sellers to find property leads.
  2. 🧮 Analyze each deal. The 70% Rule says your max offer = 70% of the After-Repair Value minus repair costs.
  3. 🤝 Negotiate a contract below your max offer — the gap is your profit.
  4. 💸 Assign the contract to a cash buyer and collect your wholesale fee (usually $10k–$15k!).
  5. 📈 Reinvest in upgrades, unlock pricier markets, and climb the ranks.

💡 You never buy the house — you flip the contract. Low risk, fast cash!
